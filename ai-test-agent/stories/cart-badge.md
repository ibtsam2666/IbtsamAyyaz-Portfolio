# Story: Cart badge shows how many items are in the cart

As a shopper, I want the cart icon to show how many items I have added, so I always know what is in my cart.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Adding a product from the inventory page increases the cart badge by one.
2. Removing a product from the inventory page decreases the cart badge by one.
3. When the cart is empty, no badge is shown.
4. The badge count matches the number of items listed on the cart page.
5. The count is kept when the shopper reloads the inventory page.
